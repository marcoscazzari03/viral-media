Privacy Policy

Viral Streamers Daily (US Viral Publisher) · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Who we are

Viral Streamers Daily is a media page that publishes short clips about streamers and viral internet moments on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube. "US Viral Publisher" is the private tool we use to publish our own videos to our own accounts.

What data we process

  • Our own accounts only. The tool connects exclusively to accounts owned by Viral Streamers Daily. It does not offer login to the public and does not access other people's accounts.
  • Access tokens issued by the platforms (Meta, TikTok, Google) are stored encrypted in our automation system and used only to publish our videos and read the statistics of our own posts.
  • Post statistics (views, likes, shares, watch time) of our own posts, used to understand which videos work.
  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or followers.

Public content

Clips are discovered from public sources (for example Twitch clips). Each video credits the original creator. Creators who want a clip removed can contact us and we will remove it.

Retention

Rendered video files are deleted from our server automatically after 7 days. Post statistics are kept as long as the page is active. Access tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected.

Third-party platforms

Publishing happens through the official APIs of Meta (Instagram, Facebook), TikTok and Google (YouTube); their own privacy policies apply to content once it is published there.

Contact

For privacy questions or removal requests, send a direct message to @viralstreamersdaily on Instagram or TikTok.
